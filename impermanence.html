Project Case Study

Impermanence

3D Boss Fight Game

Overview

Impermanence is a single-player 3D boss fight game set in Diyu, the Chinese hell, inside a dimly lit Chinese opera hall. The player controls a mafia boss who died during a failed revenge plot and is condemned for his crimes. To earn redemption and reincarnation, he must face a demon punisher known as the Wuhan moth demon and confront his guilt.
