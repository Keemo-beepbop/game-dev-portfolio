Project Case Study

Total Defense Simulator

2D Tower Defense Game

Overview

Total Defense Simulator is a Singapore-themed 2D tower defense game made in Unity using C#. Players defend Singapore from waves of aliens using towers inspired by Singapore’s six pillars of Total Defense, along with powerful spells. Towers must also be repaired after being active for too long, adding another layer of strategy.
